About BioMind

Why I built this, how it works, and what I learned.

Why I built BioMind

BioMind started as my attempt to turn biology from static notes into something interactive. I wanted a tool where students could move through concepts step by step, test themselves endlessly, and actually feel their intuition growing.

How BioMind works

Each module uses the same engine: slides and questions are stored in structured data files. The app loads them dynamically, so I can keep adding content without changing the core code. This reflects how I think about learning: modular, iterative, and always expandable.
